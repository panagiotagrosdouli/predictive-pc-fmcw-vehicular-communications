# Paper-1 Novelty Positioning

Paper 1 should use comparative, mechanism-level novelty wording only.

Defensible positioning: a causal end-to-end evaluation that connects vehicle-motion forecasts to future directional optical-link estimates and then to packet-level scheduling outcomes, explicitly examining when predictive service ordering helps, becomes neutral, or hurts, and constraining reordering with a current-service guard.

Do not claim first use or invention of trajectory prediction, predictive scheduling, deadline-aware scheduling, vehicular resource allocation, PC-FMCW, phase coding, DPSK, vehicular VLC, queueing, paired bootstrap, Wilcoxon/Holm procedures, or communication-aware learning.

The contribution is strongest when framed as the evidence-backed translation from prediction accuracy/information to downstream packet utility and the identification of service-order opportunity-cost mechanisms, under an explicit synthetic/model-based boundary.
