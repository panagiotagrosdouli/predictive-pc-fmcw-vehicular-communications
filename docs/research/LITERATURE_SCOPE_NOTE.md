# Literature Scope Note

The literature audit establishes that predictive vehicular resource decisions and PC-FMCW are prior art. Paper 1 should therefore avoid categorical novelty claims. The manuscript may position its contribution comparatively as an end-to-end causal mechanism study connecting trajectory forecasts to directional modeled optical-link states and packet-level service decisions, with explicit current-service guarding and operating-region failure/null analysis.

Verified examples include phase-coded FMCW waveform/transceiver and coherent MIMO radar work by Kumbul et al.; the PC-FMCW laser-headlamp ISCAI work by Liu et al.; reliability/latency-aware vehicular resource allocation by Guo, Liang, and Li; trajectory-prediction-assisted V2V task offloading by Guo, Rui, and Gao; and predictive vehicular resource allocation by Lu et al. Exact DOI metadata and safe citation uses are recorded in `REFERENCE_VERIFICATION.md`.
