# Paper-1 Claim Audit

## Supported only with corrected evidence

Paper 1 may claim that the repository implements a causal trajectory→geometry→modeled-link→packet scheduling chain and evaluates whether future mobility/link information changes packet-level utility. Any numerical superiority statement is conditional on the corrected-deadline revalidation artifact and its frozen statistical protocol.

## Historical evidence

Workflow run `34055071988` is reproducible historical evidence: its 80 holdout rows independently reproduce the versioned means, percentile-bootstrap confidence intervals, paired Wilcoxon tests, Holm adjustments, and paired Cohen dz values. It is nevertheless **pre-correction evidence** because the packet deadline service window was one slot too long and its nominal 0.05 s deadline was not representable with 0.1 s slots. Those values must not be copied into a corrected manuscript as if unchanged.

## Allowed framing

- Causal predictive scheduling mechanism study under synthetic/model-based assumptions.
- PC-FMCW/DPSK-informed reference-SNR optical link abstraction.
- Oracle as evaluator-only perfect-future information reference, not a deployable or globally optimal policy.
- Conditional HELP/NEUTRAL/HURT language when directly supported by the corrected paired evidence.
- Explicit failure/null regions and latency/fairness trade-offs.

## Prohibited framing

- Universal predictive superiority.
- Measured, calibrated, road-tested, hardware-in-the-loop, or real-world optical-channel validation.
- First invention of PC-FMCW, phase coding, trajectory prediction, predictive scheduling, deadline-aware resource allocation, or vehicular optical communications.
- Packet/time-sample pseudoreplication; the frozen inferential unit is the paired episode/seed within regime.
- Paper-2 learned-model claims before the complete WOMD/checkpoint/held-out/OOD evidence exists.

## Submission gate

External-submission wording is not final until corrected workflow results, updated tables/abstract/conclusion, current CI, LaTeX compilation, PDF inspection, and final provenance manifest all agree. Author/affiliation placeholders are also a submission blocker that requires real author metadata rather than inference from the repository account.
