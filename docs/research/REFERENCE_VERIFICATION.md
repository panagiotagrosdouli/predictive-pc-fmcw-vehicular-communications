## Claim boundary

The literature supports only comparative novelty wording. Paper 1 must not claim first use of trajectory prediction, predictive scheduling, deadline-aware vehicular allocation, phase coding, FMCW, PC-FMCW, or optical vehicular communication. The defensible contribution is narrower: a causal trajectory→link→packet evaluation of a PC-FMCW/DPSK-informed directional optical scheduler, with explicit service-order mechanism analysis and HELP/NEUTRAL/HURT operating regions.
